# AVRMD Product Specification

## Product statement

**AVRMD — Medical XR Network** helps healthcare and immersive-technology professionals find trustworthy organizations, solutions, evidence, projects, and events, then connect with verified owners and peers.

AVRMD uses “medical XR” as the umbrella term. VR, AR, and MR remain explicit filters; “MX” is retained only as a search alias.

## Launch audience and boundaries

- Global-English and professional-first.
- Public catalog browsing; free member and owner accounts.
- No diagnosis, treatment recommendations, clinical consultation, patient records, or personal health information.
- No owner payment is required to claim a page.
- Paid promotion is visibly separated from editorial and organic discovery.

## Core user outcomes

1. A visitor can find a relevant solution using natural-language search or structured filters.
2. A professional can evaluate why a result matches, inspect linked evidence, save it, compare it, and contact its verified owner.
3. A company or organization can claim an operational entity and maintain its official facts, updates, events, and inquiry routing.
4. A researcher or editor can correct evidence metadata without turning scholarly records into marketing pages.
5. AVRMD staff can review submissions, claims, rights, community reports, advertisements, and AI suggestions through auditable queues.

## Initial record types

- **Organization:** company, hospital, university, laboratory, nonprofit, association.
- **Solution:** software, hardware or gear, device, service, platform.
- **Evidence:** study, scholarly paper, report, article metadata, case study.
- **Event:** conference, webinar, workshop, meetup.
- **Project:** research initiative, grant, open-source project, implementation program.
- **Group:** professional or topic community.

## Trust model

Badges are independent signals:

- **Claimed owner:** an operational entity has an approved representative.
- **Source reviewed:** AVRMD staff checked the cited source and record fields.
- **Community submitted:** the record originated from a member.
- **AI-assisted:** AI proposed one or more fields.
- **Human reviewed:** an editor approved the current revision.
- **Sponsored:** a paid placement; never an endorsement or evidence grade.

No badge implies clinical effectiveness, safety, or regulatory approval.

## MVP exclusions

Patient communities, personal health advice, direct messaging, online ad payment, full procurement/RFP workflows, unrestricted web crawling, autonomous publishing, multilingual UI, behavioral advertising, and separate per-item microsites are deferred.
