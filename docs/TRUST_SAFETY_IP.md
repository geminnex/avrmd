# Trust, Safety, and Intellectual Property

## Content collection

- Store factual metadata, original AVRMD summaries, citations, and outbound links.
- Do not reproduce full articles or abstracts unless the license expressly permits it.
- Do not display third-party logos or media on unclaimed pages.
- Require an ownership or license attestation and rights record for every uploaded asset.
- Preserve source URL, publisher, retrieval date, license, rights basis, and affected fields.
- Respect machine-readable rights reservations, source terms, robots rules, and access controls.

Fair use is contextual and never represented as a guaranteed excerpt length.

## Notice and takedown

Before public production launch, AVRMD will publish:

- a copyright contact and designated-agent information;
- notice, counter-notice, removal, restoration, and appeal procedures;
- a repeat-infringer policy;
- an evidence-preservation and audit procedure.

## Medical safety

- AVRMD provides professional information, not medical advice.
- Patient records, personal health information, diagnosis, individualized treatment, and clinical consultation are prohibited.
- Regulatory status is region-specific, dated, and source-linked.
- Claimed-owner, sponsored, and source-reviewed labels never imply clinical endorsement.
- Evidence labels describe source type and review status, not safety or effectiveness guarantees.

## Owner and community independence

Verified owners manage official facts, licensed media, team access, updates, FAQs, and inquiry routing. They may answer or report community content but cannot remove independent evidence, questions, or criticism. AVRMD moderators adjudicate reports and appeals.
